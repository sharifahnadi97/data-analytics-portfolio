# Healthcare Provider Dashboard

## Overview

This project analyses healthcare provider data to understand billing, treatment costs, patient activity, departments, procedures, diagnoses, and patient satisfaction. The analysis was built using Excel and Power BI, with a focus on transforming healthcare data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualisations and KPIs.

## Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

- Analyse overall billing and healthcare costs
- Compare billing amounts across procedures and departments
- Analyse billing by city
- Understand billing patterns across different diagnoses and service types

## Key Visualisations

### Billing by City

A map visual showing the geographical distribution of total billing amounts across cities in the United Kingdom.

### Billing by Procedure

Compares total billing amounts across procedures such as:

- X-Ray
- CT Scan
- MRI Scan
- Ultrasound
- Blood Test

### Billing by Diagnosis and Service Type

Breaks down billing amounts across different diagnoses and service types, including:

- Hypertension
- Appendicitis
- Asthma
- Fracture
- Migraine

Service types include Emergency, Inpatient, and Outpatient.

### Billing by Department

Compares total billing amounts across departments, including:

- Cardiology
- Orthopedics
- General Surgery
- Neurology
- Pediatrics

The percentage contribution of each department to the overall billing amount is also calculated.

## Key Insights

Some key observations from the dashboard include:

- X-Ray generated the highest billing amount among the listed procedures.
- Cardiology contributed the largest share of total departmental billing.
- Treatment costs represent the largest component of the overall billing amount.
- Outpatient services contributed a significant proportion of billing across several diagnoses.
