# Coffee Shop Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyses coffee shop sales data from two different datasets to explore sales performance, customer purchasing patterns, product performance, and sales trends. Although the datasets contain different records and structures, both analyses focus on similar business questions, such as identifying popular products, understanding sales patterns, and evaluating overall performance. The project demonstrates the use of Excel to clean, analyse, and visualise sales data, with the findings presented through tables, charts and interactive analysis.

## Tools Used
* Microsoft Excel: Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Excel formulas, Data cleaning and transformation, Data visualisation

## Objectives
* Analyse coffee shop sales performance
* Identify best-selling products and categories
* Explore sales trends over time
* Analyse customer purchasing patterns
* Compare product and category performance
* Identify useful trends and patterns in the data
* Present business findings clearly using Excel

## Analysis
The project covers areas such as:

* Total sales and revenue
* Sales by product
* Sales by category
* Sales trends over time
* Product performance
* Order and transaction analysis
* Customer purchasing patterns
* Peak sales periods
* Comparative analysis of different sales datasets

## Key Insights
The analyses were used to identify differences in sales performance, product demand, and purchasing patterns across the datasets. The findings help highlight which products and categories contribute most to sales, when sales activity is highest, and where noticeable patterns or trends can be observed.
