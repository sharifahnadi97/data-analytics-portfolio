# Hospital Emergency Room Analytics Dashboard

## Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard analysing Emergency Room patient data to provide insights into patient volume, admission patterns, waiting times, referrals, demographics, and patient experience. The dashboard provides both consolidated and monthly views to help identify patient flow patterns and operational trends.

## Objectives

- Monitor overall patient volume and admission status
- Analyse patient waiting-time performance
- Identify referral patterns by department
- Understand patient demographics
- Analyse patient distribution by day and hour
- Monitor patient satisfaction
- Compare monthly and overall performance

## Tools Used

- **Power BI** – Data modelling, DAX, dashboard development and visualisation
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **Excel** – Data preparation and initial analysis

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Monthly View
Provides an overview of patient activity for the selected month, including:

- Total patients
- Average wait time
- Patient satisfaction score
- Number of patients referred
- Admission status
- Patient age and gender distribution
- Referral distribution by department
- Patient distribution by day and hour
- Waiting-time target performance
- Patient race distribution

### 2. Consolidated View
Provides an overall view of Emergency Room activity across the full analysis period, allowing users to identify broader trends and patterns.

### 3. Patient Details
Provides a detailed view of individual patient records and their characteristics.

### 4. Insights
Summarises key descriptive findings and operational observations from the analysis.

## Key Insights

- **9,216 patients** were recorded across the analysis period.
- **4,612 patients (50%)** were admitted, while **4,604 (50%)** were not admitted.
- The average patient waiting time was **35.3 minutes**.
- Only **41% of patients were seen within the 30-minute target**, while **59% exceeded the target**.
- **3,816 patients** were referred, with General Practice and Orthopaedics accounting for the highest referral volumes.
- **Saturday** recorded the highest overall patient volume among the days of the week.
- Patient satisfaction averaged **4.99**, indicating a generally positive patient experience despite waiting-time challenges.
